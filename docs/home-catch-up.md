# The home page catches up with the airship

Ari, 2026-11-15, after the airship hero shipped: "yay good airship!!! but what about literally everything else? :) the rest of the ouroboros.bot homepage needs to catch up. you may change literally everything!"

## Who it's for

Someone Ari met while he represented Microsoft's Customer Innovation Team: customer builders already doing real work with agents, and Microsoft product owners. They give the page a fraction of a second. They should leave trusting that Ari is the best at working with agents and at building tools for them, and excited to build with him.

## What stays (approved)

The headline "How can humans and agents do more together?", the intro, the airship, Agent view, the family bar and footer, and the product families (Desk, Ouro MD, Ouro UI, m365crawl, Spoonjoy; Friends, Shepherd and Butler inside Ouroboros).
